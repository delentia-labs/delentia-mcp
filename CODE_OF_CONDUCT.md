# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, email **founder@delentia.com**. Reports are handled privately.
